5. Return & Refund Policy

7-Day Guarantee

We stand behind the quality of our home furnishings. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may initiate a return within 7 days of delivery.

5.1 Return Eligibility Criteria

  • The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original pristine condition.
  • Original tags, brand packaging, fabric labels, and purchase receipt must be intact.
  • Custom-tailored or clearance sale items are non-returnable unless defective upon arrival.

5.2 Refund Processing

Once returned items pass quality inspection at our warehouse (typically 24-48 hours from pickup), refunds are processed as follows:

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI/Cards/Net Banking): Refund credited back to original payment source within 3-5 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Refund transferred to your verified bank account or Ferikart wallet within 24 hours.
